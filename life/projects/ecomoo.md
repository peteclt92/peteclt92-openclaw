# eComOO (eCommerce Outcome Orchestrator)

**Type:** project
**Status:** active
**Last updated:** 2026-01-28

## TL;DR
A merchant-side, glass-box control plane for outcome bundles: improve profit/conversion today while enforcing explicit rules and generating audit-friendly traces.

## Confirmed
- Core idea: sell the "right setup" (bundles) under deterministic policies, with traceability + replay + hot-fixes.
- EU-first thesis: governance becomes a buying requirement sooner (AI Act / GDPR expectations).
- Not a consumer assistant, not a marketplace, not a generic black-box recommender.

## Proposed (unverified)
- First platform: Shopify-first.
